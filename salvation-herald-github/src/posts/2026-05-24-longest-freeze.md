Salvation has seen hard winters. Old-timers still measure them against the storm of '61. But no one on the square can remember a May like this one.

The county road has been impassable for eleven days. Deliveries have stopped. The grocers on Main have gone to rationing flour and lamp oil, and the churches have opened their halls to families whose woodpiles ran out weeks ago.

## The Lake Holds Its Silence

Strangest of all is Cauldron Lake, frozen solid and utterly still. The fishermen who work its north shore say they have never known it to fall so quiet — no wind on the ice, no birds, nothing.

Whatever is coming, the Herald can only report what the town already knows: that this is no ordinary winter, and that no one can say when it will end.
